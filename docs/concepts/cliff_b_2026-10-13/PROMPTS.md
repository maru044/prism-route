模式：内置 image_gen，非 CLI。输出均为不透明背景。概念首稿生成，未执行 Rhino 建模或游戏材质替换。

Roof: convert the right plateau's pale-green cap into a cohesive near-future white roof landscape, with the existing raised oval mass becoming a low flowing white pavilion/roof island, a small recessed skylight and walkable roof terraces integrated into the forms. Keep white architecture low and horizontal; preserve adjacent terrain silhouettes and existing broad routes, without inserting a new city or giant unrelated tower. Chalk-white mineral composite / fine limestone-concrete appearance, matte warm off-white, faint neutral cool and warm variations and extremely subtle evenly scaled mineral grain like a seamless world-space triplanar material. No UI, no Rhino viewport labels, no red construction curves, no text, no watermarks.
