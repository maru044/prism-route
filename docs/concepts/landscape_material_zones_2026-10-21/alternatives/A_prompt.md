Crucial design objective: an assertive, coherent Zaha Hadid landscape composition that reads as a large continuous FIELD of directional forces at the scale of the site. The ground graphics are the architecture. This is not a municipal park with curvy sidewalks linking blobs of lawn. Avoid pedestrian path networks, oval flowerbeds, generic serpentine garden paths, plaza islands, scattered tree-dot grids, meandering loops, and decorative clutter. Express strong controlled asymmetry, gradual changes in direction, compression and expansion, a few carefully composed families of curves, large quiet counterspaces. All geometric marks flow into their surrounding field instead of being isolated ornaments.
Material palette across all versions: chalky warm white and pale grey concrete/limestone remnants, subtle weathered joints, slightly eroded edges and restrained moss seams; grass, low heath and silver-green scrub with two or three compatible tones; dark asphalt and turquoise sea. Show aged near-future infrastructure being reclaimed by low plants. At this aerial scale, tree canopies must be sparse, clustered at a handful of locations and secondary to the material pattern. No golf-course landscaping. No rubble piles or excessively cracked paving, neon colors or colorful ornamental flowerbeds. Rich fine-scale material detail but very clear organized large-scale geometry. Calm soft daylight, no haze hiding the pattern. One unified full-site image with no labels, no legend, no UI, no watermarks.

DESIGN A — FLOW LAMINATION / INTERWOVEN STRATA. This version should have the strongest continuous directional flow. Across each of the existing large plateaus, create five to eight elongated, closely related but NON-EQUALLY-SPACED laminae of planting and pale ruin surface, like an aerodynamic field wrapping an island. Bands vary broadly in width (roughly 8–45 m), run for hundreds of metres, merge into adjacent bands, locally split once or twice, and taper into long precise tips. White and green are interleaved sheet-like fields, not white narrow routes surrounding green oval beds. Roughly 55–60% low planting and 40–45% exposed pale architectural ground on the largest tops. Some broad pale ribbons should narrow into thin residual seams as neighboring green sweeps converge; a few green sweeps should widen into large calm fields. Orient the dominant flowing pattern diagonally from southwest to northeast through the entire site, deflecting around the lagoon, road junctions and edges rather than looping concentrically around every lawn. The upper-right long peninsula carries a striking long set of accelerating diagonal sweeps; the lower central horseshoe roof has THREE long ribbons that smoothly curl with its existing contour and slide past the oval pavilion. Carry the directional family through the existing planted terrace levels. Expose the same visual flow in fine paving seam alignment. Trees remain minimal. Visually reference the spatial continuity of Zaha Hadid's BEKO landscape and the force-field organization of Hoenheim-Nord, reinterpreted as low planted/material strata, not a street network. The result should look sculpted by flow even though the ground surfaces themselves stay at their existing levels.
